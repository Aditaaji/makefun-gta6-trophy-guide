    Sources & honesty notes

    Names, descriptions and grades come from the PlayStation list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles, cross-checked on 19 November 2026 against GTA Zone, PowerPyx, GTABase, Planet GTA 6, Output Lag, KeenGamer, SixShore and Push Square — all agreeing on every name, description and grade. This is not an official Rockstar list: those sites label it Reported, because Rockstar has published no official list. Five trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) have unconfirmed triggers: their guides say so plainly and invent nothing.
